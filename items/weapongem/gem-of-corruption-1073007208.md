## Description

Hitting an enemy applies a  mark that can stack up to 5 times.  Marked enemies will emit an explosion when no new stack of  mark has been added for %k or if the enemy dies. The explosion deals %k shroud damage in a 1m radius. Each stack adds 0,5m and %k of the base damage to the explosion.
