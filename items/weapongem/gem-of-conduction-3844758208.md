## Description

Hitting an enemy puts a  mark on them for %k. Hitting a  marked enemy sends a lightning to another  marked enemy, that deals %k and can chain to futher marked enemies. After a lightning went off, the effect has a %k cooldown before it can trigger again from the same enemy. Enemies that are part of a lightning chain have their  mark refereshed.
